Health and wellbeing

This is not a medical device and it gives no advice. It counts what you told it to count. A score is a record of what you tapped, not a measurement of you.

1Turning it down

Streaks and divisions are there to make keeping something going feel worth doing. When one stops helping, none of this costs you your record:

Archive a habitIt leaves the grid and stops being scored. The history is kept
Skip daysTurn it on in Settings. A skipped day holds the score and the streak where they are
Ignore the ArenaThe grid works on its own. Nothing on it needs a division
