Delete Your Habit Nemesis Account

You can permanently delete your Habit Nemesis account and its associated account data directly from the app.

How to delete your account
  1. Open the Habit Nemesis app.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Open the Account section.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

What happens when you delete your account?

Deleting your account removes your Habit Nemesis account and associated data stored for your account. The deletion action is intended to permanently remove the account rather than simply signing you out.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or cannot complete the deletion process, please contact the Habit Nemesis developer through the contact information provided on the app's privacy policy page.
