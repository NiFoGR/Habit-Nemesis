2What the app is, and what it is not

The app is a habit tracker. You list habits, mark days, and the Arena plays your week against the best week you have already had. What you mark is stored on your device. An account is optional and adds a copy held for you, so the record survives a new phone.

One thing to know about Android: the operating system's own backup can copy the app's data to your Google account, so it comes back when you reinstall. That is an Android setting, between you and Google, and section 7 of the privacy policy explains how to turn it off.

It is none of these:

  • A medical device.
  • Medical, mental health, fitness, financial or legal advice.
  • A replacement for a doctor, a therapist or any other professional.
  • A safe way to time medication, or anything else where a late or missing reminder does harm.

Streaks, scores, divisions and feats are a game built on what you type in. They measure your entries. They do not measure your health, your discipline or your worth.
