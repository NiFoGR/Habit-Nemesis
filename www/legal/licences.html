3What does not ship

Listed for completeness, because people reasonably expect to see it. None of this reaches your device.

  • @capacitor/cli. Builds the Android project on a developer's machine. MIT.
  • The Android toolchain. Gradle, the Android SDK and the Java compiler run at build time only.
  • The app's own tooling, under tools/: the development server, the icon generator, the two checks and the build patches. Written for this app and part of it.

One package in the build is ours and is not open source: habit-nemesis-systemui, a small Capacitor plugin under native/ that hides the Android navigation bar. It is covered by section 6.

4Fonts

There are none, and that is deliberate. The app asks for the system font stack and uses whatever the phone already has, so nothing is downloaded and no font licence applies. On an iPhone that is San Francisco, on Android it is Roboto, and in a browser it is whatever the operating system provides.

5Artwork

Under review

The division crests, the seasonal cups and the share-card backgrounds in www/img/ are images, and their provenance is being established before the app is published. They are not asserted to be original work and they are not asserted to be licensed.

Their status is recorded in the app's own documentation, in docs/ART.md, and replacing them is a listed blocker in docs/RELEASE.md. This page will name the artist and licence for each once that is done.

The app icon is a placeholder drawn by the app itself, not an image file.

Every other visual element is drawn in code: the icon set is inline SVG in www/js/icons.js, and the charts, rings and bars are drawn by the app.
